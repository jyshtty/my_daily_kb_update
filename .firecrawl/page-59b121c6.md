**Process** \\
\\
I applied through a recruiter. The process took 3 weeks.\\
\\
Team: Organizations\\
\\
- Initial call with recruiter\\
- 30-minute interview with the hiring manager\\
- Coding assessment. Seven-day due date, three days to submit once started\\
- Tech interview with two engineers (staff + senior)\\
\\
The next day, I got the email saying they were going to proceed with another candidate.\\
\\
**Questions** \\
\\
HM interview:\\
\\
- Why the change?\\
- What do you know about the company and the role?\\
- A successful project and a failed project to talk about\\
- Process for learning something new.\\
- Process of teaching someone something new.\\
Be ready to talk about projects and mentoring.\\
\\
Home Assessment:\\
\\
- CodeSignal Rate Limiter exercise\\
\\
Tech Interview:\\
\\
- Reviewed the CodeSignal and asked questions. No live coding, no enhancement asks, just clarification about decisions.\\
- Totally project focused. Be prepared to talk a lot about a big project, database focused, query performances, your role in the project, how you handled differences and communication with other teams.\\
